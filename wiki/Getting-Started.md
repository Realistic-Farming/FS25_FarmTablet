# Getting Started

This page walks you through opening the tablet for the first time and learning the handful of controls. There is not much to learn, the whole point is that it gets out of your way.

---

## Opening and closing

Press **T** to open the tablet. Press **T** again, or **ESC**, to close it. That is the main control.

T is just the default. If it clashes with another mod or you would rather use a different key, you can change it. See [[Settings]] for how, or use `TabletKeybind` in the [[Console Commands]].

The tablet remembers what it was doing. It opens to your chosen startup app, at the size and position you left it, every time.

---

## It works like a phone

The whole tablet is built to feel like a phone or tablet you already know. There are three screens it moves between: a **lock screen**, a **home screen** of app icons, and the **app** you have open.

```
 LOCK  ->  HOME (app grid)  ->  APP
 slide      tap an icon         tap HOME to go back
```

## The lock screen

The first time you open the tablet in a session you get a lock screen. **Slide to unlock**, just like a phone: click and drag the knob across the track. It is a small bit of flavour and it only takes a second, then you land on the home screen.

If you would rather skip it, turn the lock screen off in [[Settings]]. With it off, the tablet opens straight to the home screen.

## The home screen

The home screen is a grid of glossy app icons, exactly like a phone springboard.

- **A dock across the bottom** holds four favourites that are always there: Dashboard, Weather, App Store, and Settings.
- **Page dots** sit above the dock when you have more apps than fit on one page. With companion mods loaded you will have a few pages.
- **A home indicator** bar sits at the very bottom, the little strip you would swipe on a real tablet.

## Getting around

| What you want | How |
|---------------|-----|
| Open an app | Tap its icon. It zooms open to fill the screen |
| Go back to the home screen | Tap **HOME** in the top bar of any app |
| Move between home pages | Mouse wheel over the grid, or click a page dot |
| Scroll an app's content | Mouse wheel over the app |
| Open the in app help | Tap the small **i** button, bottom right of the app |
| Close the tablet | Press T again, or ESC |

So the loop is simple: open the tablet, tap an app, read what you need, tap HOME, and either jump to another app or press T to get back to farming. Every icon shows the app's full name underneath it, so you can read the grid at a glance and tap the one you want.

---

## The i button is your friend

Every app has a small **i** button in the bottom right corner of its content area. Tap it and you get a plain English help page that explains everything that app shows, including what every colour and bar means. Tap **BACK** to return.

I wrote those help pages so you never have to leave the game to understand an app. This wiki is the same information in one place, but the in game help is always right there if you forget.

---

## Your first look around

A good first tour:

1. **Dashboard** opens by default. This is your money, your fields, your vehicles, the time, and the weather, all on one screen.
2. Tap **Weather** for the full forecast.
3. Tap **Field Manager** to see every field you own and what state it is in.
4. Tap **App Store** to see the full list of apps you have, grouped by category. Anything dimmed is a companion app you could unlock by loading that mod.
5. Tap **Settings** when you want to move the tablet, change the open key, or pick a different startup app.

When you are comfortable, read [[The Apps]] for the full rundown, or [[Customising the Tablet]] to make it sit exactly where you want.

---

## Changing what opens first

By default the tablet opens to the Dashboard. If you would rather it open to, say, Weather or Field Manager, set a different startup app in [[Settings]] or with `TabletSetStartupApp`. Small thing, but if you always check the same screen it saves a click every single time.
