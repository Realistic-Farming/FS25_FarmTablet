# Core Apps

These four apps are always present. They are what makes the tablet a tablet: your overview, your app launcher, your settings, and your changelog.

---

## Dashboard

**App id:** `dashboard`

This is the home screen and the app that opens first by default. It gives you the whole farm in one glance so you rarely need to open anything else for a quick check.

**What it shows**

| Section | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Current Balance | Your total available money. Green when positive, red when overdrawn. Shows your loan amount alongside if you have one. |
| Income / Expenses / Net P/L | Tracked since you loaded this session. Income is money earned, expenses is money spent, Net P/L is the difference. |
| Active Fields / Vehicles | Fields you own that have a crop growing, and the count of motorised vehicles your farm owns. |
| Active Contracts | How many contracts you currently have in progress. Open the Contracts app for the detail. |
| Season / Day / Time / Weather | The in game clock and current conditions. Season only shows if a Seasons mod is active. |

**Customising the widgets**

The Dashboard is yours to arrange. Tap the small **EDIT** button in the top right and you can choose which widgets appear and in what order. If you never look at one of them, switch it off and keep the screen clean. Your choice is saved.

## App Store

**App id:** `app_store`

A directory of every app registered with the tablet, grouped into Core, Farm, Finance, and Mod Integrations.

- Every app shows its name, developer, and version. Built in apps show "Built-in", companion apps show "Integrated".
- Tap **OPEN** on any row to jump straight to that app. It is a shortcut for finding and tapping its icon on the home screen.
- The Mod Integrations group lists every companion app the tablet knows about. Active ones show in full colour with an OPEN button. Dimmed rows are supported but the mod is not loaded, so you can see at a glance what you would unlock by adding it.

Nothing here needs setup. Companion apps appear on their own when their mod is present.

## Settings

**App id:** `settings`

One scrollable panel for everything you can configure. This is where you move the tablet, change the open key, pick your startup app, choose a background, and toggle sounds.

Because there is a lot here, it has its own page. See [[Settings]] for the full reference, and [[Customising the Tablet]] for moving and reskinning it.

The quick version of what lives here: display position and scale, Enter Edit Mode, background colour, custom home background, the sound toggles, startup app, notifications, debug mode, and Reset All To Defaults.

---

## Updates

**App id:** `updates`

The full changelog for Farm Tablet, newest version first. Each block shows the version number and the list of what changed in it.

It is the fastest way to see whether you are on the latest build and what you would get by updating. Grab new versions from the [Releases page](https://github.com/Realistic-Farming/FS25_FarmTablet/releases), drop the zip in, and your settings carry over.
