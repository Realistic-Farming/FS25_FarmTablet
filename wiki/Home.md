# Farm Tablet

**Your whole farm, one key press, zero menus.**

Welcome to the Farm Tablet wiki. This is the full manual for the mod, written by me, the person who built it. If you just want to get going, jump to [[Installation]] and you will be running in about ten seconds. If you want to understand every app and setting, everything is here.

---

## What is Farm Tablet?

Farm Tablet is a heads up command centre for Farming Simulator 25. Everything you normally dig through menus to find is put on one clean, full screen tablet. You open it, check what you need, and get back in the cab. No pausing, no menus stacked four deep, no alt tabbing to a spreadsheet.

I built it because I was tired of pausing the game just to see my balance, then pausing again to check the weather, then walking to every pen to see if the animals were fed. I wanted one place that showed me everything. So that is what this is.

The tablet only reads your game data. It never changes your farm state behind your back, so there is no risk of save corruption or mod conflicts. The few apps that can change things (like Farm Admin) are clearly labelled and admin gated in multiplayer.

---

## The short version

1. Drop the zip in your mods folder.
2. Enable Farm Tablet on the mod screen.
3. Load your save and press **T**.

That is it. Settings live inside the tablet itself and save instantly. There is nothing to configure in a text file.

---

## What is inside

Farm Tablet ships with **20 built in apps** and adds **12 more apps automatically** when it detects a companion mod in your save. That is up to 32 apps, all one tap away on the home screen.

- **Core apps** keep the tablet itself running: Dashboard, App Store, Settings, Updates.
- **Farm apps** cover the day to day: weather, fields, animals, vehicles, storage, contracts, production, and more.
- **Mod integration apps** appear on their own when you load one of my other mods. You do not configure anything.

See [[The Apps]] for the full list with a one line summary of each.
