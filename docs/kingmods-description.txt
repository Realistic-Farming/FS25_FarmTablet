FarmTablet: a complete farm management tablet for Farming Simulator 25.

Open it with one key and your whole farm is right there. A phone style tablet with a lock screen, a home screen full of glossy app icons, a dock for your favourites, and as many pages of apps as you need. Tap an app, check what you need, tap home, and get back in the cab. No pausing, no menus stacked four deep, no alt tabbing to a spreadsheet.

Originally inspired by a tablet mod for FS17, built from scratch for FS22, and now fully rewritten from the ground up for FS25. The goal is still simple: useful farm info at a glance, without getting in the way. Every app lives in its own file, so the mod is easy to expand, fix, and improve, and it is actively shaped by community feedback.


What is in it right now (20 built in apps):

Dashboard: farm overview with balance, income, fields, vehicles, time and weather, with widgets you can arrange
Weather: current conditions, temperature, wind, cloud cover and a five day forecast
Field Manager: every owned field with crop type and growth state
Animals: pen status with food, water and cleanliness levels
Workshop: nearby vehicle diagnostics with fuel, wear and hours, plus one click repair
Digging: live position, terrain height and dig depth
Bucket Tracker: load counter for wheel loaders and excavators
Storage: silo inventory and the best sell price per crop
Time Controls: set the time scale and skip to a time of day
Hotspot Manager: view and clear map pins
Notes: a simple farm todo list
Farm Admin: money, time and vehicle controls (host only)
Field Jobs: log your field work sessions
Contracts: active contracts with completion, reward and time left
Fleet Manager: every owned vehicle sorted by fuel
Production Buildings: your production chains, inputs and outputs
Farm Stats: a full farm statistics snapshot
App Store: browse every installed app, grouped by category
Settings: configure the whole tablet from inside the tablet
Updates: changelog and version history


Mod integrations (auto detected at runtime, safe to run without them):

NPC Favor, Soil Fertilizer, Field Sentry, Seasonal Crop Stress, Income Mod, Tax Mod, Market Dynamics, Worker Costs, Personnel (Pro-Staff), Random World Events, UsedPlus, and Invoices (with RoleplayPhone). Load any of these mods and its app appears in the tablet on its own. No setup, no load order to worry about.


Made yours:

Move, resize and recolour the tablet, set your own home screen image, choose which app opens first, and arrange the Dashboard exactly how you like. Everything saves with your game.


Other details:

Full multiplayer support, every player gets their own tablet and the actions that change the game are admin gated
Dedicated server safe, the tablet is client side and skips the server with no overhead
Reads your game data only, it never changes your save
Ships in 26 languages and follows your game language


The mod is in active development. Bug reports, ideas and suggestions are always welcome. This is a community project and your feedback directly shapes what gets built next.
